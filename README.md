# Case Acceptance Report
### SQL · Excel · Healthcare Analytics · Business Intelligence

## Overview

Developed a **Case Acceptance report** to measure how effectively doctors convert diagnosed treatment into accepted procedures.

Because many dental procedures are elective, the report focused on understanding which doctors were strongest at **diagnosing treatment and getting patients to move forward**, including whether treatment was accepted the same day or at a later date.

## Analysis

- Developed complex SQL logic to calculate case acceptance metrics
- Built Excel reporting using **pivot tables and formulas**
- Compared individual doctors and offices against **overall averages and benchmarks**
- Analyzed acceptance rates by **doctor, office, diagnosis, and procedure**
- Identified doctors with the highest treatment acceptance rates by procedure
- Compared same-day acceptance to later treatment acceptance
- Identified **missed treatment opportunities** where diagnosed procedures were not accepted

## Doctor Performance

The report provided individual offices with visibility into how doctors performed relative to their peers and the overall average.

This helped identify **which doctors were strongest at diagnosing and converting treatment**, as well as areas where additional diagnosis or treatment acceptance could create opportunities for the practice.

## Impact

Created a consistent way to evaluate **doctor-level case acceptance and missed treatment opportunities** across offices.

The report became one of the primary reports distributed to leadership and individual offices to help identify performance gaps and opportunities to improve treatment conversion.
